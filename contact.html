SCU Students

If you are a current SCU student and wish to meet with me, you are welcome to attend my office hours in Bergin 104. You can find my office hours posted on my office door or inquire with the department administrators.

I have a limited number of research opportunities for motivated undergraduate and MS students. If you are interested in a project, please email me your resume. It is generally easier for me to work with students who have already taken an AI-related course such as CSEN 140, 166, 240, 272, 281, 342, or 346.

Prospective Students

I am always open to working with highly motivated PhD students. My research interests are outlined here. I encourage you to read or skim some of my recent publications to understand the technical depth and application areas of my recent projects.

If you are interested in working with me, you can apply to SCU's PhD program in Computer Science and Engineering. Please mention my name specifically in your personal statement when you submit your application.

Due to the high volume of emails I receive from prospective students, I may not be able to respond to all inquiries. I appreciate your understanding and apologize if I am unable to reply.
